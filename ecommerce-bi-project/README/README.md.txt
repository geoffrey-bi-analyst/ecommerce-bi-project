# 📊 E-commerce Sales & Customer Insights Dashboard

## 🧩 Project Overview
This project analyzes e-commerce data to generate insights into sales performance, customer behavior, and product trends. The goal is to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Track key business KPIs (Revenue, Orders, Customers)
- Analyze regional performance
- Identify top-performing products
- Understand customer distribution

---

## 🧰 Tools & Technologies
- Google BigQuery (Data warehouse & SQL)
- Power BI / Looker Studio (Data visualization)
- SQL (Data modeling & transformation)

---

## 🗄️ Data Modeling
The data was structured into:
- Fact table: Orders (revenue, timestamps)
- Dimension tables: Customers, Products
- Final analytical view combining all datasets

---

## 📈 Key Metrics
- Total Revenue: 20.31M
- Total Orders: 99K
- Total Customers: 95K
- Average Order Value: 205.83

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Revenue is highly concentrated in a few regions
- Top products contribute a large share of total revenue
- Customer distribution is uneven across regions
- Low repeat purchase behavior observed

---

## 💡 Recommendations
- Improve customer retention strategies
- Expand into underperforming regions
- Focus on high-performing products
- Stabilize revenue beyond seasonal spikes
